# Glossary

Canonical terms used in AORAFORGE methodology and reports.

## AIO

Google AI Overviews, measured from the search experience rather than from a public API.

## Citation

A deterministic parser decision that a response includes the registered brand name within the agreed scope. The default scope is the answer paragraph; customers may choose a looser full-response rule at pre-registration.

## Check-in

A scheduled polling point relative to publication: Day 7, 14, 21, and 30 by default. Day 45 is the verification point for refund decisions.

## Example Status

The public/private label attached to example data:

- `synthetic`: created for documentation and validation.
- `anonymized-real`: derived from real polling with customer-identifying details removed.
- `customer-approved-public`: real customer data approved for public use.

## Platform Independence

The rule that ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Brave Search are reported separately. Cross-platform summaries may help navigation, but substantive claims use per-platform rates and intervals.

## Pre-registration

The Day-0 lock on target queries, competitors, brand-name match rules, and citation scope. Pre-registration prevents cherry-picking after results are known.

## Wilson 90% Confidence Interval

A two-sided 90% Wilson score interval around a binomial citation rate. AORAFORGE uses Wilson instead of the naive Wald interval because Wilson remains better calibrated for small samples and boundary cases.
